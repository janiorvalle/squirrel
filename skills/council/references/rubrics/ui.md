# Rubric: UI

For a screen, a page, a component, a flow. Seven categories, each scored 1 to 10, then Overall, your judgment of the whole, not an average.

**Typography.** Type sizes form a scale with a reason for each step. Weights carry meaning. Line length and line height make the body readable. Labels and values are distinguishable at a glance. Fallback fonts don't show.

**Hierarchy.** The eye lands on the most important thing first, then the next. Page title, section, row, control: each sits at its own level. Nothing shouts that shouldn't. Nothing important recedes.

**Spacing and density.** Padding and gaps come from one scale. Rows and cards are as dense as the task allows and no denser. Empty space is placed, not left over. Columns are sized to their content.

**Color and contrast.** A small palette with a job for every color. Brand color marks what matters. Status colors are distinguishable and consistent. Text meets contrast on every background. Neutrals are few.

**Iconography.** One icon set, one stroke weight, one size per context. Each icon reads without its label. Icons that mean the same thing look the same everywhere.

**Consistency and polish.** Corner radii, borders, shadows, button shapes and control sizes repeat. The same element looks the same on every screen. Alignment holds to a grid. No stray borders, no off-by-one paddings, no mixed button styles.

**Clarity.** Someone who uses this screen can tell what each thing is, what state it's in, and what they can do next, without reading documentation. Copy is in the user's words, not the system's. Controls say what they do.

**Overall.** Would you show this screen to a designer as an example of the category done well? 10 means yes without caveats.
